# E006 results: SEA-RAFT flow bias in crowd velocity variance (CrowdFlow, 2 Oct 2026)

Status: six rendered crowds tested (IM01, IM01_hDyn, IM03, IM05, plus new IM02, IM04; IM01 and hDyn are the same crowd, so five independent crowds). PERSON-LEVEL reference from the CrowdFlow trajectories. From TRUE start points, classical Farnebäck flow with a 1-4 frame direct gap gives raw bias within +-10% in four crowds (worst 9.6%, 8.8%, 6.4% for N = 1, 2, 4), with no gain needed, and the result is unchanged under added noise (std 8 of 255) and JPEG quality 30 (worst 9.1%, Cell 23). SEA-RAFT, DIS and RAFT-large shrink more, and SEA-RAFT shrinks further under JPEG/noise in IM05 and IM01. The slow crowd IM04 fails under every flow method tested (Farnebäck -25% to -31%, SEA-RAFT -40% to -90%, DIS -49% to -60%, RAFT-large -40% to -63%), so the pre-declared five-crowd gate FAILS. Local image texture does NOT explain IM04 (Cell 24, declared check not supported); the cause is unexplained. Realistic start-point errors break the 1.5 s estimator (Cells 14-17). Pixel-seeded advection (no detections) not supported. Not yet tested on real video; all data are rendered (synthetic).

## Setup
- Data: TUB CrowdFlow (synthetic/rendered, exact ground-truth flow, academic use). Subsets of the first 120 files of images, gt_flow (60 .flo + 60 .png), masks per scene. IM01 and IM01_hDyn show the SAME crowd (dynamic camera). Independent crowds: IM01(+hDyn), IM03, IM05, IM02, IM04.
- Verified: flow is forward k -> k+1 as stored; mask > 0 marks people (covers 99-100% of moving pixels, ~7% of the frame). hDyn has real camera drift of ~2-3 px/frame in the images, but its ground-truth flow of the BACKGROUND is ~0; only the people pixels of hDyn are valid.
- Model: SEA-RAFT checkpoint MemorySlices/Tartan-C-T-TSKH-spring540x960-M, 4 iterations, full resolution, Tesla T4. Other methods (Cells 21-24): OpenCV Farnebäck (pyr_scale 0.5, levels 5, winsize 15, iterations 3, poly_n 7, poly_sigma 1.5; fixed, not tuned), OpenCV DIS medium preset, torchvision RAFT-large (12 updates, default weights).
- Pixel-level Var(v) proxy: spatial variance of flow over crowd pixels in 80x80 patches (sum over x and y), after averaging the flow over s x s windows. It mixes between-person and within-person (limb) motion.
- Trajectory files: gt_trajectories/<scene>/personTrajectories.txt. One line per person, one pair per frame stored as (y, x) (NOT x, y), pair (0,0) = absent. People: 180 / 191 / 694 / 1340 / 260 / 307 for IM01 / hDyn / IM03 / IM05 / IM02 / IM04. Trajectory column k-1 corresponds to image frame k in all six crowds (offset -1; median step vs GT flow 0.05-0.10 px). Verified: 99.4-100% of points land on people.
- Approximate body size (mask area / people, equivalent diameter): IM01 27 px, hDyn 26 px, IM03 11 px, IM05 17 px, IM02 23 px, IM04 15 px.

## Findings (pixel proxy)
1. SEA-RAFT works: EPE ~0.7 px vs 2.5-3.9 px for zero flow. Raw stated variance is unusable for this checkpoint (var_min = 0, sigma^2 >= 2 px^2 per axis).
2. Pixel proxy: noise (error spread) ~80% of true Var(v), cross term -93 to -107%, net bias -11% to -26% at pixel level, -22% to -40% at 8-32 px windows.
3. Even subtracting the TRUE error variance (oracle) makes the estimate worse (-37% to -73%): "subtract a learned noise variance" (original C1) is not supported.
4. A fitted gain works in-scene but does NOT transfer across crowds against the pixel proxy (best gains 1.16-1.55; gate fit IM01 -> new scene: 2 of 6 cells pass). Ground-truth-free signals (flip-TTA disagreement, speed, crowd fill, texture) do not predict the gain (|r| <= 0.26).

## Findings (person level, 160 px patches, >= 8 people per patch, one-frame steps)
5. Within-patch slope of SEA-RAFT flow on the real step: IM01 0.92, hDyn 0.92, IM03 0.90, IM05 0.78. Var(v) bias (1 px | 5 px sampling): IM01 -4.3 | -9.5%, hDyn -0.5 | -6.2%, IM03 -11.3 | -15.5%, IM05 -10.9 | -17.1%. Correlation 0.98-0.996 (IM05 0.88). Best gain 0.98-1.11 (1 px), 1.04-1.16 (5 px).
6. Leave-one-crowd-out (IM01 held out together with hDyn; one gain fitted on the other crowds): worst held-out |bias| 8.6% (1 px) and 9.0% (5 px) vs raw worst 11.3% / 17.1%: PASS of the pre-registered +-10% gate on the first three crowds only (before IM04 was added).
7. Decisive sampling-vs-patch-size test (same patch sizes 80/120/160/240 px): the pixel estimator stays at -23% to -49% at 240 px; the person estimator is -1.6%, +0.3%, -11.3%, -12.8% at 240 px. The way Var(v) is sampled, not the patch size, explains the gap. Mechanism (limb smoothing) is still a hypothesis.
8. Time windows (Cell 11, N = 1..16 frames, truth = (x_{k+N} - x_k)/N):
   - True person-velocity variance falls with the window: N=1 -> 16: IM01 0.683 -> 0.448, hDyn 0.575 -> 0.486, IM03 0.583 -> 0.533, IM05 0.832 -> 0.409. The window length must be stated with any Var(v) or P value.
   - "Chain" estimate (average of N one-step flows at TRUE positions): bias small and does not grow with N in the first four crowds.
   - "Direct" estimate: fine up to N=2-4, then fails in dense scenes (IM01 N=16 +80%; hDyn N=16 +174%; IM05 N=16 +137%); IM03 stays fine.

## Decisions / consequences
- C1 is reframed from "learn a per-pixel flow error variance and subtract it" to "calibrated, window-aware measurement of flow-derived person-velocity variance". The effect is known in PIV (spatial-resolution attenuation; box averaging reproduces it), so novelty must be framed as the application to crowd video and learned flow, plus the sampling / start-point / slow-crowd findings; novelty check for these not done.
- Supported so far (TRUE starts, rendered images, clean or mildly degraded): a short-gap (1-4 frames, 0.04-0.16 s) direct Farnebäck estimator is within +-10% raw in four independent crowds with no calibration; learned flow (SEA-RAFT, RAFT-large) and DIS shrink more and are less stable under degradation. The window length must be stated.
- NOT supported: a general Var(v) within +-10% across all five crowds (IM04 fails for all four flow methods), a single gain across crowds (it does not help Farnebäck), learned flow as a fix for slow crowds, local texture or contrast as the explanation of IM04, a texture-based reliability flag, detector-based starts, detection-free pipelines, any claim about real video.
- The IM04 failure is itself a result: crowd pressure matters most in dense slow crowds, which is where flow recovers least motion. Cause unexplained. Lead (hypothesis, one crowd, post-hoc): people with close neighbours lose motion in IM04.
- A reliability flag from the video alone is not yet supported (texture fails; predicted-displacement and neighbour-count flags rest on one slow crowd).
- Multiple comparisons: ~48 start/estimator variants per window in Cells 14-17; 6 cells in Cell 22; 8 cells in Cell 23; 15 gaps in Cell 24. Only pre-declared candidates tested on crowds not yet used for choosing them count; for Farnebäck that is IM01+hDyn and IM03 only.

## Caveats
- Five independent rendered crowds from one engine; CrowdFlow is synthetic with clean frames; no real-video ground truth; transfer to real video untested (compression, noise, perspective, frame rate and pixel scale differ).
- Cells 11-13 and 18-24 start from TRUE person positions; detector stand-ins (Cells 14-17) are synthetic and untuned. Farnebäck from realistic starts untested.
- The 10% / 15% gates, 4 px displacement rule, 0.85 recovery and 0.10 texture gap are my own proposals; patch counts per crowd are modest (46-171 patches).
- IM04 and IM02 were first seen in Cell 18; Cells 19-24 reuse them, so they are exploratory.
